# OFFICIAL SCHOLARSHIP POLICY: NATIONAL MERIT-CUM-MEANS SCHOLARSHIP (SCH001)

1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW AND OBJECTIVE
The National Merit-Cum-Means Scholarship (SCH001) provides financial assistance to meritorious undergraduate students from economically weaker families to help them pursue higher education without financial distress. The scholarship awards INR 50,000 per academic year.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
To qualify for this scholarship, applicants must satisfy all of the following requirements:
- Enrolled full-time in an accredited undergraduate program (Academic Years 1, 2, 3, or 4).
- Open to students of all social categories (General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS).
- Minimum cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of 7.50 on a 10.0 scale, or equivalent percentage in previous academic terms.
- Total annual gross family income from all sources must not exceed INR 2,50,000 (Rupees Two Lakh Fifty Thousand only).

3. APPLICATION PROCESS AND DEADLINE
Applications must be submitted online through the Central Scholarship Portal. The annual deadline for submission is October 31, 2026. Late or incomplete applications will be rejected automatically without consideration.

4. MANDATORY REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
Applicants must upload clear, scanned copies of the following authentic documents:
- Official Family Income Certificate issued by a competent Revenue Authority (e.g., Tahsildar or Sub-Divisional Magistrate), issued within the current financial year.
- Certified semester marksheets/transcripts of all previous qualifying examinations.
- Front page of the student's active Nationalized Bank Passbook showing Account Number, IFSC code, and linked Aadhaar number.

5. RENEWAL CONDITIONS
The scholarship is renewable annually across all 4 years of study provided:
- The scholar maintains a minimum CGPA of 7.50 at the end of each academic year with no active backlogs or standing arrears.
- Regular annual attendance of at least 75% is maintained across all courses.
- Income certificate is re-verified at the beginning of each renewal cycle.
- The scholar maintains good disciplinary standing with no institutional misconduct records.

6. GRIEVANCE AND APPEAL PROCESS
Applicants whose applications are rejected or who face discrepancy in disbursement may submit a formal written appeal to the Student Welfare Appeals Board within 15 calendar days of notification. Appeals must include supporting evidence, application registration ID, and institutional endorsement. The Board will review the grievance and issue a binding resolution within 30 days.
