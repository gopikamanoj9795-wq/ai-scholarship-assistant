# OFFICIAL SCHOLARSHIP POLICY: APEX TECH ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE FELLOWSHIP (SCH003)

1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW AND OBJECTIVE
The Apex Tech Academic Excellence Fellowship (SCH003) is a prestigious merit fellowship established to recognize and nurture top-tier academic and engineering talent in undergraduate institutions. The fellowship provides an annual grant of INR 75,000 along with mentorship access to senior industry researchers.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
Applicants must satisfy all stated benchmarks:
- Currently enrolled as a 3rd Year or 4th Year undergraduate student in Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronics, or allied engineering and tech disciplines.
- Open to students of all demographic and social categories.
- Exceptional academic record with a minimum cumulative CGPA of 8.50 out of 10.0 (or equivalent top percentile standing).
- Annual household income must not exceed INR 8,00,000 (Rupees Eight Lakhs only).

3. APPLICATION TIMELINE AND SUBMISSION
Applications must be submitted via the Apex Fellowship Management Portal by 11:59 PM on December 1, 2026. Submissions require completion of an online personal statement explaining academic goals, research interests, and project experience.

4. MANDATORY SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS
Every candidate must supply high-resolution scans of:
- Official university marksheets and transcripts reflecting all terms through the end of the 2nd Year (for 3rd-year applicants) or 3rd Year (for 4th-year applicants).
- Comprehensive technical Resume / Curriculum Vitae highlighting software projects, internships, publications, or hackathon achievements.
- Exactly two Letters of Recommendation written and signed by department faculty members or project advisors.
- Valid Institute Student Identity Card.

5. RENEWAL GUIDELINES
Fellowship renewal for the 4th Year requires:
- Maintenance of an exemplary CGPA of at least 8.50.
- Completion of a technical capstone or research project milestone during the 3rd Year, evaluated and approved by the Faculty Mentorship Committee.
- Submission of an Annual Technical Progress Report by May 31.
- No history of academic integrity infractions or disciplinary sanctions.

6. APPEALS AND RECONSIDERATION POLICY
Candidates who believe their submission was evaluated incorrectly due to administrative oversight or transcript transmission errors may submit a formal request for reconsideration to the Apex Fellowship Review Committee within 10 calendar days of status announcement. Appeals must include a cover letter detailing grounds for review and endorsement from the Head of Department. All appellate determinations are final.
