# OFFICIAL SCHOLARSHIP POLICY: STATE OBC HIGHER EDUCATION FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE (SCH002)

1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW AND OBJECTIVE
The State OBC Higher Education Financial Assistance scheme (SCH002) is a targeted financial aid initiative designed to uplift Other Backward Classes (OBC) students pursuing undergraduate degree programs. The total financial benefit is fixed at INR 40,000 per academic year disbursed directly to the beneficiary's bank account.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
Candidates must fulfill each of the following mandatory conditions:
- Belong to a recognized Other Backward Classes (OBC / Non-Creamy Layer) category with valid state certification.
- Currently enrolled in Year 2, Year 3, or Year 4 of an undergraduate degree program. (First-year students are not eligible for this specific state scheme).
- Minimum cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of 7.00 across all preceding semesters.
- Combined annual parental/household income from all verified sources must not exceed INR 3,00,000 (Rupees Three Lakhs only).

3. APPLICATION PROCEDURE AND DEADLINE
All applications must be lodged through the State Higher Education Department Portal on or before November 15, 2026. Applicants must ensure their Aadhaar card is seeded with their primary bank account before finalizing the submission.

4. REQUIRED VERIFICATION DOCUMENTS
The applicant must submit digital copies of:
- Official Community / Caste Certificate issued by an authorized Revenue Officer (Tahsildar level or higher) confirming OBC Non-Creamy Layer status.
- Valid Annual Family Income Certificate.
- Certified marksheets of all completed undergraduate semesters to date.
- Domicile Certificate proving residency in the state.

5. RENEWAL CONDITIONS
To continue receiving the financial assistance in subsequent eligible academic years:
- The student must clear all enrolled subjects in each examination session without active academic backlogs.
- The student must sustain a minimum annual CGPA of 7.00.
- Mandatory institutional attendance of at least 75% in every semester.
- Submission of an annual continuation certificate endorsed by the College Principal or Dean.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND APPEAL PROCESS
In case of application rejection, delayed stipend disbursement, or technical discrepancies during verification, candidates may file an appeal through the State Scholarship Grievance Cell within 14 business days of result declaration. The appellate committee conducts a comprehensive re-audit of uploaded certificates and issues an official decision within 21 working days.
